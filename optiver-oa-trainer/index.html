Optiver OA Trainer

Free, open-source practice for the Optiver online assessment — probability, sequences, likelihood ranking, interval estimation, and orderbook arbitrage. Runs entirely in your browser, no login, no tracking.

Note: This is an unofficial study tool, not affiliated with Optiver. The Beat the Odds format closely follows public descriptions; the reasoning games are reconstructions built to train the same underlying skills. Real assessment UIs and exact timings vary by role and cycle.

Beat the Odds — Probability

141 verified questions across 11 categories: dice, coins, cards, expected value, gambler's ruin, Bayes, recursion/"game resets", hard multi-concept mixes, and an expert set. Orderbooks

Spot riskless arbitrage across products and their bundles (A, B, C, A+B, A+B+C). Buy at asks, sell at bids, find the max profit — or call no-arb. Procedurally generated, infinite rounds.

NumberLogic

Sequences (fractions left unsimplified, interleaved series, second differences, Fibonacci-style operations), row-rule grids, and hidden-operation puzzles. Infinite generation.

Likelihood List

Read a histogram, scatter plot, or table and rank 3 statements from most to least likely. +1 / −1 / skip 0 scoring, with true percentages revealed after each round.

Intervals
